📊 Executive Sales & Revenue Control Center Dashboard

An enterprise-grade, interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide key business metrics, financial trends, and multi-dimensional sales analysis for data-driven decision-making.

---

🚀 Key Features

* Executive KPI Panel:** Real-time tracking of critical business metrics including Total Revenue ($26K), Total Profit ($7K), Average Unit Pricing ($4K), and Units Sold (103).
* Dynamic Time-Series Analytics:** Integrated Combo Chart (Line + Clustered Column) mapping revenue and profit trajectories across custom date ranges.
* Product & Regional Breakdown:** Horizontal bar chart highlighting top-performing revenue drivers paired with a Donut Chart showing regional market share.
* Cross-Dimensional Performance Matrix:** Advanced Category × Region matrix table enabling fast cross-tabular revenue and profit exploration.
* Interactive Control Panel:** Multi-level slicers for Region, Product Category, and Date Range filtering.
* Modern UI/UX Design:** Built with a custom dark executive theme, structured container-bordered elements, high contrast, and clean visual hierarchy.

---

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used

* Business Intelligence : Power BI Desktop
* Data Modeling & Visualization: Power Query, DAX, Custom Matrix & Combo Visuals
* Design & Theme: Custom Dark Theme
